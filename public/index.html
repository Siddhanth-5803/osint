Step 1

How to export your chat

Choose "Without media". It's smaller and we don't look at photos anyway.

WhatsApp on Android
  1. Open the chat or group.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right), then More, then Export chat.
  3. Pick Without media.
  4. Save the .zip to Files or Drive, then choose it here.
WhatsApp on iPhone
  1. Open the chat and tap the name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  3. Pick Without Media.
  4. Choose Save to Files, then choose that .zip here.
Telegram (desktop app)
  1. Open the chat, click ⋮, then Export chat history.
  2. Untick photos and videos, set format to Machine-readable JSON.
  3. Drop the result.json here.
Instagram DMs
  1. Settings, then Accounts Centre, Your information and permissions, Download your information.
  2. Pick Messages, format JSON.
  3. When the download is ready, open messages/inbox/<chat>/ and drop message_1.json here (or the whole zip).
